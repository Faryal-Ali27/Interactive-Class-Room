# 50 Problem Statements → Solutions (Traceability Matrix)

Source: the "Interactive Classroom" problem statement slide (NZB – Jinnah University for Women).
Every row is solved by a feature that exists in this project.

## A. Teacher cannot see understanding in real time
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | No reliable way to know in real time if students understand | Live results dashboard (bars update instantly) |
| 2 | "Does everyone understand?" gets no honest answer | Anonymous Quick Poll: Got it / Somewhat / Lost me |
| 3 | Only loudest, most confident voices are heard | Every student answers at the same time on their own device |
| 4 | Quiet or struggling students stay invisible | Students tab: shows who answered, who is confused, who is waiting |
| 5 | Gaps found only after a graded test | Live quiz questions during the lesson |
| 6 | Feedback arrives days or weeks late | Results appear the moment students tap |
| 7 | Teacher can't tell how many students are lost | Confusion meter (😕 count and bar) |
| 8 | Teacher can't tell which topic the class struggled with | Report tab: correct % per question |
| 9 | Teacher can't tell who has not responded yet | "x of y answered" counter and student list |
| 10 | Teacher can't tell if the pace is right | Pace feedback: Too fast / Just right / Too slow |

## B. Teacher cannot adapt teaching
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 11 | Cannot adjust pace during the lesson | Live pace counters on dashboard |
| 12 | Cannot re-explain at the right moment | Close & reveal shows the correct answer and class split immediately |
| 13 | Opportunity to correct course is lost | Per-option percentages while the topic is still fresh |
| 14 | Cannot offer targeted support | Students tab highlights confused and non-responding students |
| 15 | Lessons planned on assumptions about readiness | Pre-lesson readiness poll from the question queue |
| 16 | Wasted instructional time | Timers and one-click Exit Ticket |
| 17 | Uneven learning outcomes | Report shows weak questions to revisit |
| 18 | Pressure on teachers to grade | Automatic grading and scoring |

## C. Students have no voice
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 19 | Confused students are reluctant to speak up | Anonymous "I'm confused" button |
| 20 | Fear of asking in front of peers | Anonymous Q&A |
| 21 | True state of the class stays hidden | Answers are hidden from peers, aggregated for teacher |
| 22 | Raising hands is biased toward confident students | Everyone submits an answer |
| 23 | Shy students have no channel for longer thoughts | Open text answers |
| 24 | Problems are only caught in formal assessments | Continuous low-stakes checks all lesson |
| 25 | Students can't give feedback on the lesson | Star rating and Exit Ticket |
| 26 | Students can't react without interrupting | Emoji reactions (👍 ❤️ 👏 😮 😂) |
| 27 | Peers' good questions never surface | Upvoting in Q&A |
| 28 | Students don't know where they stand | Instant correct/wrong feedback and leaderboard |

## D. Tools are too complex, costly or disconnected
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 29 | Tools are too complex | Join with a 4-digit code, no signup |
| 30 | Tools are too expensive | Free, open source, self-hosted |
| 31 | Tools are disconnected from the live lesson | Live updates (polling every 2–3 s), question sent in one click |
| 32 | Installation is required | Runs in any browser, no app |
| 33 | Student accounts are required | Nickname only |
| 34 | Setting up questions is slow | Question queue and Exit Ticket preset |
| 35 | Teacher must retype the same quiz | Queue saved in the browser and on the server |
| 36 | Poor phone experience | Mobile-first student UI with bottom navigation |

## E. Assessment is slow and limited
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 37 | Paper-based quizzes are slow to grade | Auto-grading |
| 38 | No instant insight from answers | Instant charts |
| 39 | Right/wrong feedback is delayed | Reveal on close, highlighted correct answer |
| 40 | Cannot time-box questions | Per-question timer (15s to 2min) |
| 41 | Only one question format available | 6 types: MCQ, True/False, Poll, Rating, Word cloud, Open text |
| 42 | Can't gauge free-text understanding at scale | Word cloud aggregation and open-text list |
| 43 | No record of the session | CSV report download |

## F. Engagement and moderation
| # | Problem | Solved by |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 44 | Class stays passive | Points for correct answers |
| 45 | Low motivation to participate | Leaderboard with speed bonus |
| 46 | Many text answers are unreadable | Word cloud sizes words by frequency |
| 47 | Inappropriate or off-topic questions | Q&A moderation (review before showing) and delete |
| 48 | A few students dominate discussion | Anonymous voting on questions |
| 49 | Answered questions clutter the list | Mark as answered / reopen |
| 50 | Dashboards are hard to read or tiring on the eyes | Minimal white dashboard with dark/light theme toggle |
